Deterministic Architecture

The 6-Stage Autonomous Compliance Pipeline

From raw PDF ingestion to tamper-evident cryptographic log — fully deterministic execution after extraction.

STAGE 01

Contract Ingestion

Parses native PDF & DOCX agreements with regex fallback for clause splitting and section hierarchy.

STAGE 02

LLM Clause Extraction

Translates clauses into strict 6-field structured JSON records (actor, deadline, target, expected state).

STAGE 03

Dependency Graph

Maps upstream prerequisite clauses to downstream conditions, calculating ripple risk across obligations.

STAGE 04

Multi-Source Evidence

Ingests emails (IMAP), Google Sheets, and ERP telemetry with mathematically weighted trust levels (0.4 - 1.0).

STAGE 05

Risk & Dispute Engine

Computes target deviation, trust deficit & deadline proximity. Opens 48h counter-evidence dispute window.

STAGE 06

Hash-Chain Escalation

Logs unresolved breaches into an immutable SHA-256 hash-chained log with one-click cryptographic tamper verification.
